Remediation: ECM rebuild scope is restricted to package-consumed inputs only: `packages/kde/extra-cmake-modules/*`, `scripts/run-kde-ecm-package-preflight.sh` and `.github/workflows/kde-ecm-package-preflight.yml`. DAG state/evidence, documentation and selector-only edits must scope-skip.
